## [1.0.0] - 2026-06-19

### Added

- Two-step QR creation flow (URL entry → design).
- Live QR styling: logos, body patterns, corner styles, gradients, frames.
- Dynamic and static QR code support.
- Management dashboard with search, type filter, infinite scroll (shimmer loading), duplicate, and delete.
- Multi-format export (PNG, JPEG, SVG, WEBP).
- Google / email sign-in screen (prototype).
